 THIS MONTH’S CAREER QUESTION

How do I interview for a job
I haven’t done before?

Start with the experience you do have. Choose two responsibilities from the job description and prepare a real example of how you’ve used a related skill.
 
HERE’S HOW THAT COULD SOUND
“ “I haven’t worked in event sales, but I’m comfortable helping customers make a choice. At the restaurant, I explained menu options, answered questions, and helped resolve concerns.”

Use your own experience. Explain what you did and what happened. If a task is new to you, ask:

What would training cover, and what should I be able to do independently when I start?
 
 
 PREPARE FOR YOUR NEXT INTERVIEW

Make your experience count.

Choose one responsibility from the job description. Prepare a real example that explains:
1 What happened
2 What you did
3 The result

Customer-facing work? Describe how you helped someone compare options or resolve a concern.

Hands-on work? Explain how you followed instructions, caught an error, or solved a problem.
